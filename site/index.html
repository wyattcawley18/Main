The diner on 160

Small-town feel, home-cooked plates.

Fort Mill Family Restaurant is a small-town diner on SC-160, and you can smell breakfast cooking before the door swings shut behind you.

There’s breakfast — biscuits and gravy, chocolate chip pancakes, three eggs sunny-side up, livermush and cheese. And there are plates — grilled pork chops, marinated beef tips, fried shrimp and flounder, chicken and waffles.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same things: good-size portions, a friendly staff and food that comes out fast. Eat in, take it to go, or have it delivered.
