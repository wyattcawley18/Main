The place

A neighborhood bar & grill on Meeting Street.

Bedford Falls is the kind of place you settle into. Pull up a stool at the bar or grab a table on the outdoor patio, order something good from the kitchen, and let a friendly, attentive crew take it from there.

Prices are reasonable, the drinks are made with care, and the kitchen stays open late — so the night doesn’t have to end when you get hungry.
